Privacy Policy

Effective date: 4 October 2026  ·  Last updated: 4 October 2026 (services added before release; identifier wording; App Tracking Transparency)

Short version. Arena Hoops has no accounts and never asks for your name, email or contacts. Your progress stays on your device. The game shows ads between matches and optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob (with Unity Ads competing inside AdMob), and offers one optional one-time purchase, Remove Ads, handled by your app store and RevenueCat. Gameplay statistics and crash reports (Google Firebase) are off until you choose ALLOW.

1. Who we are

Arena Hoops is a mobile basketball game developed by Prime Quantum Studios, an independent game developer based in London, United Kingdom. "We", "us" and "our" refer to Prime Quantum Studios. This is a general location, not a registered company address, and no company registration is claimed here.

Privacy questions and requests, support requests, and data access or deletion inquiries: primequantumstudios@gmail.com.

2. What the game itself keeps on your device

Your career progress and stars, coins, fans, the balls and training you have unlocked, daily gift and mission progress, your sound, vibration and statistics settings, and the cached Remove Ads status are stored only on your device. We do not receive them. Uninstalling removes them from the device, although a device or cloud backup that you have switched on may keep a copy.

We do not sell your data. The services below act as our service providers or, for the advertising partners, also for their own advertising, measurement and fraud-prevention purposes. 4. Categories of data, in plain terms

  • Approximate location — inferred from your IP address by the advertising partners and (after ALLOW) Firebase. The game never requests GPS or precise location.
  • App interactions — app launches, taps and ad views (advertising partners), and gameplay events (Firebase, after ALLOW).
  • Diagnostics — ad SDK performance information such as launch time, hang rate and energy use, and (after ALLOW) crash reports.
  • Purchase history — only if you buy or restore Remove Ads. Every match can be played without paying.
  • Device or other identifiers — the advertising identifier and other device identifiers provided by the operating system, and random per-installation identifiers used by Firebase and RevenueCat.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, calendar, microphone, camera, precise location or browsing history outside the game. The game has no chat and no free-text input, and its statistics accept only a fixed list of events with numbers, so personal details cannot be logged by mistake.

5. Your statistics choice, and why ads are separate

Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics are switched off on a clean install. After your first win the game asks once, with equal ALLOW and NOT NOW buttons. Nothing is collected before you allow it. You can change your mind any time with STATS on the main screen. Turning it off stops statistics right away; Google documents that switching crash reporting off may only take effect from the next launch. This switch controls Firebase only — it does not stop advertising data processing, which follows your ad choices below.

6. Consent and how ads are personalised

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regulated regions): Google's User Messaging Platform asks for your choices before ads are requested. Where this applies, you can review or change your choices at any time with PRIVACY CHOICES on the game's main screen.
  • US states with privacy laws: where Google's tools show an opt-out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal information for targeted advertising, it is available through the same choices.
  • iPhone and iPad (App Tracking Transparency): before ads are personalised, the game shows a short explanation and then Apple's own prompt asking whether you allow tracking. If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the advertising identifier is not shared and ads are not personalised with data from other companies' apps and websites. You can change this any time in your device's Settings under Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Device settings: you can reset or delete your advertising identifier or opt out of ad personalisation in your device's privacy settings.
  • Ads only appear between matches, never during play, and their content is limited to a PG maximum rating. Rewarded ads are always optional. Buying Remove Ads stops the ads between matches. The game is not directed at children.

7. Retention and security

Data on your device stays until you delete it or uninstall the game (subject to your own backups). Data held by the services in section 3 is kept under their own retention rules and settings. We keep no servers and no database of our own for this game.

8. Children's privacy

Arena Hoops is intended for a general audience aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the game, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

9. International transfers

The services in section 3 are operated by companies based in the United States and may process data in the United States and other countries, under their own safeguards and transfer mechanisms.

10. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port personal data, to object to or restrict processing, or to opt out of targeted advertising, under laws such as the UK GDPR, the EU GDPR and US state privacy laws. What you can do in practice:

  • Change or withdraw ad consent: PRIVACY CHOICES on the main screen (shown where it applies).
  • Stop gameplay statistics and crash reports: STATS on the main screen.
  • Reset or delete your advertising identifier, or limit ad personalisation, in your device settings.
  • Delete local game data by clearing the app's data or uninstalling.
  • Contact us at primequantumstudios@gmail.com for privacy questions, support or a data access or deletion inquiry.

What we can and cannot delete. Arena Hoops has no accounts, no login and no in-app deletion feature, we keep no database of our own, and the game does not show the random identifiers the services use. We usually cannot connect a request to one installation and cannot promise deletion on a provider's behalf; we will tell you which provider holds what and forward a request where its process allows. Purchase records are held by your app store and are also kept for its tax and accounting obligations.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy, for example if the game adds a service. The "Last updated" date above will change with it, and significant changes will be reflected in the game's store listing.
