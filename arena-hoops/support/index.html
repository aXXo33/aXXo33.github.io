How to shoot

Swipe up from the ball towards the hoop. The direction of the swipe decides left or right, and its length decides short or long. A miss tells you what went wrong (SHORT, TOO LONG, LEFT, RIGHT or RIM OUT) so you can correct the next one. A shot that is a little too long can still go in off the glass. Every make moves you to a new spot on the floor; a miss keeps you where you are.

Matches and the career

  • Score Run — reach the target score before the clock runs out.
  • Ball Rack — reach the target with a fixed number of balls.
  • Rival and The Final — outscore a computer-controlled rival team; in a final the last 10 seconds count double.
  • Makes in a row fill the crowd meter; a full house sets the ball on fire and doubles your points until you miss or the crowd cools down.
  • From the third match, every fifth ball is a rainbow money ball worth double.
  • The career has 40 matches in five arenas. Win an arena's final to open the next arena. Stars never go down, so you can replay a match to improve.

Coins, the locker and daily rewards

Coins come from matches, stars, the daily gift and three daily missions. You can also watch an optional ad to double the coins of a match, the daily gift or a daily mission. In the Locker you can spend coins on ball designs (they change the look, never the shot) and on training. Coins cannot be bought with real money.

Remove Ads

  • Remove Ads is a one-time purchase on the main screen. It removes the ads that appear between matches; the optional double-coins ads stay available because they give you something. There are no subscriptions.
  • Restore a purchase: use RESTORE PURCHASES on the main screen while signed in to the same store account you bought with.
  • Refunds are handled by the app store you bought from, not by us. After a refund the purchase is removed from the game the next time it syncs.

Ads

Ads come from Google AdMob, with Unity Ads competing inside AdMob. There are no ads during play and none in your first three matches; after that a full-screen ad may appear when you leave the results screen, at most about once every three matches (and not right after you watched a double-coins ad). Where the law requires it, your ad privacy choices can be reopened any time with PRIVACY CHOICES on the main screen.

Gameplay statistics and crash reports

These are off on a clean install. The game asks once, after your first win, and you can change the answer any time with STATS on the main screen. This switch does not stop advertising data processing — see the Privacy Policy.

Sound and vibration

Use SOUND and VIBRATION on the main screen to switch them on or off.

Progress and saved data

Progress and settings live on your device. There is no account and no cloud save, so progress does not transfer between devices. Uninstalling removes local data from the device, although a device or cloud backup you have enabled may keep a copy. The game can be played offline; ads need an internet connection.

Data access and deletion

We keep no database of our own. Data is processed by the services listed in the Privacy Policy, which also explains the controls you have. Email us with the subject "Privacy" for any data question.

Bugs and feedback

Please include what you were doing, the match number, your device model and OS version, and a screenshot or screen recording if you have one.
