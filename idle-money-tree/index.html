Idle Money Tree

Tap a tree. Grow a fortune.

An idle tycoon game about one very profitable plant — tap it, upgrade it, then let it earn while you are gone.

Download on the App Store Get it on Google Play

What it does

Twelve upgrades, two halves of one machine

Strong Finger, Golden Touch and Midas Hand make every tap worth more. Small Root, Solar Panel, Watering System, Fertilizer Plant, Drone Army, Money Rain, Gold Mine, Fertile Soil and Magic Seed earn for you whether you tap or not.

It keeps working while you don't

Close the app and the tree keeps producing, at half rate, for up to eight hours. Come back to a lump sum waiting on the screen.

Prestige when growth stalls

Cash the tree in, start the next run faster than the last one, and push the numbers somewhere the first run could never reach.

Fourteen achievements and a 2x boost

Awards mark every milestone worth hitting. Watch an optional ad and the tree pays double for sixty seconds.

Who it is for

Anyone who likes watching a number climb. Play it in bursts at a bus stop or leave it alone for a day — both are legitimate strategies, and the second one is the point.

Price

Free. The game is supported by ads, including optional ones you choose to watch for a boost.
