REBOUND: One Shot Puzzle

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 September 2026  ·  Last updated: 17 September 2026

Short version. REBOUND has no accounts and never asks for your name, email or contacts. Your progress stays on your device. The game shows ads and offers two optional one-time purchases, so ad partners, an in-app purchase service and (only if you allow it) Google Firebase process some data about your device and how you use the game. Gameplay statistics and crash reports are off until you choose ALLOW. Advertising works separately from that choice.

2. What the game itself keeps on your device

Your level progress, medals, cosmetics, settings, streaks, cached purchase entitlements and your gameplay-statistics choice are stored only on your device. Uninstalling removes them from the device, although a device or cloud backup that you have switched on (for example Android backup or iCloud backup) may keep a copy.

4. Categories of data, in plain terms

  • Approximate location — inferred from your IP address by the advertising partners, Firebase and Remote Config. We never request GPS or precise location.
  • App interactions — app launches, taps, ad views and clicks, screens and sessions.
  • Diagnostics — performance and stability information such as launch time, hang rate, energy use, and (after ALLOW) crash reports.
  • Purchase history — only if you buy or restore a purchase. Every level can be finished without paying.
  • Device or other identifiers — advertising ID / IDFA, app set ID, and random per-installation identifiers used by Unity Ads, Firebase and RevenueCat.

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, calendar, microphone, camera, precise location, browsing history outside the game, or any free-text you type. The game's analytics system only accepts a fixed list of event names and values, so personal details cannot be logged by accident.

5. Your gameplay-statistics choice, and why ads are separate

Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics are switched off on a clean install. After you complete your first level, the game asks once whether you want to share gameplay statistics and crash reports, with equally weighted ALLOW and NOT NOW buttons. Nothing is collected before you allow it, and events from before your choice are never sent afterwards. You can change your mind at any time in Settings → Privacy → GAMEPLAY STATS & CRASH REPORTS.

  • Turning it off stops analytics collection right away.
  • For crash reporting, Google documents that switching collection off may only take effect from the next time you launch the app, and that crash information recorded while collection is off is stored on your device and is sent if collection is later switched on. The Firebase SDK we use offers no way for the game to delete those stored reports, so we do not claim to delete them.
  • This switch controls Firebase only. It does not stop advertising data processing. While ads are shown, the advertising partners keep processing the data in section 3 to deliver, measure and protect ads, using non-personalised or limited ads where your consent or tracking choices require it.

6. Consent, tracking and how ads are personalised

  • Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland and other regulated regions): Google's User Messaging Platform asks for your choices before ads are requested. You can review or change them at any time in Settings → Privacy → PRIVACY CHOICES. In regions where a consent form is not required, the screen says so.
  • App Tracking Transparency (iOS): the system asks whether REBOUND may track you across apps and websites. If you say no, the advertising identifier (IDFA) is not used for tracking; ads still appear, but non-personalised or limited. You can change this in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Ad content is limited to a PG maximum rating, and the game is not directed at children.

7. Retention and security

Data on your device stays until you delete it or uninstall the game (subject to your own backups). Data held by the services in section 3 is kept under their own retention rules and settings. [The Google Analytics event-data retention period for this project is to be set and stated here by the developer.] All data these services collect is sent over encrypted HTTPS/TLS connections. We keep no separate servers and no database of our own for this game.

10. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port personal data, to object to or restrict processing, or to opt out of targeted advertising or the "sale"/"sharing" of personal information, under laws such as the UK GDPR, the EU GDPR and US state privacy laws. What you can do in practice:

  • Change or withdraw ad consent: Settings → Privacy → PRIVACY CHOICES.
  • Stop gameplay statistics and crash reports: Settings → Privacy → GAMEPLAY STATS & CRASH REPORTS.
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID, or refuse app tracking, in your device settings (section 6).
  • Delete local game data by clearing the app's data or uninstalling.
  • Contact us at primequantumstudios@gmail.com for privacy questions, support or a data access or deletion inquiry.

What we can and cannot delete. REBOUND has no accounts, no login and no in-app account or data deletion feature, and the app does not display the random identifiers described above. We therefore usually cannot connect a request to a specific installation, and we cannot promise that all data will be deleted. If you can provide an identifier that a service exposes to you, we will forward the request to the relevant provider and do what that provider's process allows. Purchase records are held by Apple or Google and are also subject to their tax and accounting obligations. [The developer's detailed deletion procedure will be described here once it is finalised.]
